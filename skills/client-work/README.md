# Client work

A consultancy's sales and delivery documents, from the first call to the release notes. Ships with a sample company, Fieldstone AI, to replace with your own.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [blueprint-builder](blueprint-builder) | A tailored top-of-funnel Opportunity Blueprint that shows a prospect where AI can move their business. |
| [discovery-call-prep](discovery-call-prep) | A one-page brief before a prospect call: who is in the room, where the deal stands, the angle and the opening question. |
| [client-follow-up](client-follow-up) | A meeting transcript becomes a follow-up email draft and a table of next actions with owners and dates. |
| [sow-scoping](sow-scoping) | Discovery notes become a Statement of Work draft with phases, deliverables, roles and assumptions, price left as XXX. |
| [demo-script](demo-script) | A timed two-minute product demo: the hook, the one live wow moment, the proof and a single ask. |
| [release-notes](release-notes) | Git history becomes release notes that read like a product update, not a commit log. |
