# Recruiting

Screening resumes in batches, selling to staffing agencies, and grading job postings. Ships with sample data to replace with your own.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [resume-batch-run](resume-batch-run) | Run a client's batch of resumes through an AI screener, tier the candidates, and give a reason for every score. |
| [staffing-outreach](staffing-outreach) | Cold outreach to staffing agencies that offers to rank one of their live batches, with two follow-ups. |
| [job-posting-grader](job-posting-grader) | Grade a job posting from the seeker's side, and keep a grader honest when it publishes about named companies. |
